How to Deposit

Follow these steps to add funds to your PESKY RECHARGE wallet.

  1. Click Deposit to open the amount form.
  2. Enter the amount you want to add to your wallet.
  3. Click Confirm Deposit to continue to Paystack.
  4. Choose a payment option shown by Paystack, such as bank transfer, card, or USSD.
  5. For bank transfer, copy the account number displayed in the Paystack checkout.
  6. Verify the account number and transfer the exact amount you entered.
  7. Complete the payment. For card or USSD, follow the instructions shown by Paystack.
  8. Return to PESKY RECHARGE and click I have sent the money if that option appears.
  9. Paystack will verify the payment and credit your wallet. Allow a short time for your balance to update.

Only send money to the account details displayed in your active Paystack checkout. Card and USSD payments do not require a bank transfer.
