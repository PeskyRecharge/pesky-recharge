How to Install on Your Phone

Select your device below for step-by-step installation instructions.

  1. 1

    Tap "Install PeskyRecharge" Above

    Open peskyrecharge.shop/download.html in Google Chrome and tap the green Install PeskyRecharge button at the top of this page.

  2. 2

    Confirm Installation Prompt

    When Chrome pops up "Install app?", tap Install. If you don't see the popup, tap the three dots (⋮) at the top-right corner of Chrome and select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

  3. 3

    Launch from Your Home Screen

    The official Pesky Recharge icon will appear on your phone menu just like an APK—no manual updates required.

  1. 1

    Open in Apple Safari

    Make sure you are viewing peskyrecharge.shop/download.html inside the Safari browser on your iPhone or iPad.

  2. 2

    Tap the Share Button

    Tap the Share icon (square with an upward arrow ↑) at the bottom bar of Safari.

  3. 3

    Select "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down the share sheet, tap Add to Home Screen, and then tap Add in the top-right corner. Pesky Recharge is now installed on your iPhone!
