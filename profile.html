How to use 2FA

  1. Install Google Authenticator from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store on your phone.
  2. Open Google Authenticator and tap the + button.
  3. Choose Scan a QR code, then scan the QR code shown above.
  4. Google Authenticator will show a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
  5. Enter the current 6-digit code here and click Verify app.
  6. Keep Google Authenticator on your phone. Use its new 6-digit code whenever PESKY RECHARGE asks for your 2FA code.
